Add photos of your handwriting

  • Plain white paper and a dark pen work best. Lined paper can confuse the letter finder.
  • Leave a finger's width between words and a clear gap between lines.
  • Take the photo straight on, in even light, with the writing filling most of the picture.
  • Any writing works. Type exactly what you wrote in the box below the photo.

Check the letters

Each box below is one thing the app found. Fix any wrong label. Use Join when one letter was split in two (like a broken k), and Skip for smudges. Joined-up letters that came out as one piece can keep a multi-letter label like the; they're saved as a whole-word sample.
